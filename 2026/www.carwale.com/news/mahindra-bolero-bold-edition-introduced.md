# Mahindra Bolero Bold Edition Introduced

- **Bronze accents on the front, alloy wheels, ORVMs, cladding, and roof rails**
- **Rear-view camera added**

The [Mahindra Bolero](https://www.carwale.com/mahindra-cars/bolero/ "Mahindra Bolero") has never been a flashy SUV, but the new Bold Edition gives it a bit of colour. The edition is a styling-led package built around a bronze theme, with no mechanical changes underneath.

On the outside, bronze accents show up on the front bumper, grille and fog lamp surrounds. The same finish carries over to the alloy wheels, ORVM caps and roof rails, while the Bold Edition also gets additional cladding along the doors. The bronze alloys and cladding are the changes you notice most from the side.

![Mahindra Bolero Front Row Seats](https://imgd.aeplcdn.com/1600x900/n/cw/ec/229952/bolero-interior-front-row-seats.jpeg?isig=0&q=75 "Mahindra Bolero Front Row Seats")

Inside, the cabin gets black leatherette seats with bronze accents, which tie in with the exterior theme. The one functional addition is a rear-view camera.

Mahindra says the Bold Edition is curated through an exclusive accessories package, so the additions come as accessories rather than as a new factory trim. The standard [Bolero](https://www.carwale.com/mahindra-cars/bolero/ "Mahindra Bolero") is priced from Rs. 8.57 lakh to Rs. 9.99 lakh ex-showroom across the B4, B6, B6(O), and B8 variants. Mahindra had also offered a Bold Edition of the Bolero last year.

The powertrain stays as before. The Bolero uses a 1.5-litre, three-cylinder diesel engine that makes 75bhp and 210Nm. It is paired only with a five-speed manual gearbox and sends power to the rear wheels.
