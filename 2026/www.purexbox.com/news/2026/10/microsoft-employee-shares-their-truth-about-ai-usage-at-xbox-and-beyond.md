"Gaming is the only industry where leaders are afraid to talk about AI."

**Original Story:** There's obviously been a lot of talk over the past few years, and particularly in the past few months, about how AI is making huge waves within the gaming industry and changing how we think about both playing and developing games.

Just a few days ago, [Asha Sharma unveiled something called Xbox Labs](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/10/asha-sharma-announces-xbox-labs-to-turn-emerging-ideas-into-new-experiences) that _appears_ to be an experimental AI division to help conjure up new ideas for the future, but her accompanying statement didn't mention AI specifically.

Now, a Senior Business Strategy Manager at Microsoft has unexpectedly shared some thoughts on the topic of AI, Xbox and the wider gaming industry, claiming that it's "time to accept" that gaming is using AI in 2026.

He even advised that he could be fired for his comments, although we certainly hope that doesn't happen!
