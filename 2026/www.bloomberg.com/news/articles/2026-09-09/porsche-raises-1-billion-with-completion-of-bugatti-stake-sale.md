# Porsche Sees Higher Margin After €1 Billion Bugatti Sale

## **Takeaways** by Bloomberg AI[Subscribe](https://www.bloomberg.com/subscriptions)

Porsche AG will generate about €1 billion ($1.16 billion) selling its stake in the venture that owns the Bugatti supercar brand as the German manufacturer tries to become leaner.

The proceeds from the transaction mean the sports-car maker, majority-owned by [Volkswagen AG](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/VOW:GR), expects a higher automotive net cash flow margin for the year. It now sees a gain of as much as 7.5% this year, up from a previous forecast of as much as 5%, it [said](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/2026/company/porsche-completes-sale-of-bugatti-rimac-43231.html "Link to source") Wednesday.
