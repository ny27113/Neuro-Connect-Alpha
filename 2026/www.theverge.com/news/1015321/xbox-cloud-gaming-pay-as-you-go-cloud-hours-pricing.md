# Xbox Cloud Gaming’s pay-as-you-go option starts at $3.99 for 10 hours

[![Tom Warren](https://platform.theverge.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/01/Tom_BLURPLE.jpg?quality=90&strip=all&crop=0%2C0%2C100%2C100&w=96)](https://www.theverge.com/authors/tom-warren)

Tom Warren

is a senior correspondent and author of [_Notepad_](https://www.theverge.com/notepad-microsoft-newsletter), who has been covering all things Microsoft, PC, and tech for over 20 years.

Microsoft announced last month that it will let [anyone access Xbox Cloud Gaming](https://www.theverge.com/news/989211/microsoft-xbox-cloud-gaming-pay-as-you-go-option) in November, without the need to subscribe to Xbox Game Pass. The new pay-as-you-go model will require players to purchase bundles of cloud hours, and now Microsoft is [revealing today](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/21/cloud-playtime-now-available-to-test-with-xbox-insiders/) that bundles will start at $3.99 for 10 hours of cloud playtime.

Xbox Insiders who don’t have a Game Pass subscription will be able to start purchasing cloud playtime today, ahead of a broader rollout next month. Insiders can access “introductory” launch pricing bundles:

All of these cloud hours will include ad-free cloud gaming, shorter wait times, and higher-quality streaming. These aren’t monthly bundles of hours either, so once you purchased a pack it remains available until the hours are used.

The pay-as-you-go Xbox Cloud Gaming option arrives just in time for _Grand Theft Auto VI_. I reported earlier this month that Microsoft has secured [_Grand Theft Auto VI_ streaming rights](https://www.theverge.com/report/1005859/microsoft-xbox-gta-6-streaming-rights), but it’s not yet clear if this will be limited to just Xbox consoles. Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball revealed [on X](https://x.com/ballmatthew/status/2107667069797802443?s=46) that Microsoft won’t be streaming _Grand Theft Auto VI_ to PCs, suggesting that the web browsers and apps that can usually access Xbox Cloud Gaming will not be able to stream _Grand Theft Auto VI_.

Microsoft also continues to test free, [ad-supported cloud gaming](https://www.theverge.com/games/969969/xbox-testing-ad-supported-streaming) for Xbox Insiders, offering up another way to stream games without Game Pass.
